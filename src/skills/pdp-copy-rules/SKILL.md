# Product page rules

## The principle

A page is not an introduction to a product, it **answers the customer's questions for them**. From first look to leaving, they care about three things: what is it, is it worth it, will I regret buying it.

Read the page first (`shopify_product`), then edit. Specs, policies and stock you did not read are questions for the user, not something to invent.

## Page structure (top to bottom)

1. **First screen**
   - Main image: the product fills 60% or more, white or a real scene, no text on the first image
   - Title: the brand word is optional; product name plus one core selling point, under 60 characters
   - Price: original, current and the discount stated plainly; a gift goes under the price
   - CTA: one line above the add-to-cart button saying what happens when it is pressed
2. **Three selling points** (not ten)
   - Each: one sentence of conclusion plus one piece of evidence (material, spec, test)
   - Each must answer a real reason people did not buy: price, shipping, size, trust, timing
3. **Objections** (pick by category, four at most)
   - Shipping: the free threshold, how many days, where you do not ship
   - Returns: how many days, who pays the postage, how to start
   - Size or spec: a table plus one line for "not sure which to pick"
   - Trust: factory, certifications, warranty, real reviews (only what the store already has)
4. **Detail images**: material, construction, use in context, one line of caption each
5. **FAQ**: the five questions support answers most often
6. **Close**: another CTA plus one line of risk reversal (the return policy, not "buy with confidence")

## What may never be written

- "Best", "number one", "top selling" without something to check it against
- Medical, efficacy or safety promises (weight loss, cures, absolutely safe)
- Invented reviews, sales figures or test reports
- Stock or delivery times you did not read
- Fake countdowns or fake limited offers

For any efficacy or compliance claim, ask the user whether it can be backed. If it cannot, it is not written.

## Language

- Write in the market's own language and voice, not translationese
- Short sentences; no paragraph longer than three lines
- Numbers beat adjectives: "18 hours on one charge" beats "long battery life"

## After the edit

1. Say what changed, why, and which metric it should move
2. Wait for the user's approval, then write it through the store tools
3. Record in memory: which angle sold which product, which FAQ removed which objection
