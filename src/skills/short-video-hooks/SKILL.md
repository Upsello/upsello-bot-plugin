# Short video hooks and shots

## The skeleton

1. **Hook (0–3s)**: one line that stops the scroll, with the picture giving information at the same time
2. **Proof (3–15s)**: demonstration, comparison, detail — show, do not tell
3. **Turn (15–25s)**: turn "looks good" into "wants it" — price, the situation, the bundle
4. **Close (last 3s)**: one action, one reason

Anything over 30 seconds: the first three seconds must stand on their own.

## Hook library

**Question**
- "Has this happened to you?"
- "Why does your [product] break after two months?"

**Against the obvious**
- "Stop buying [the common choice]"
- "Three things [the industry] will not tell you"

**Comparison**
- "What's actually different between the 20 and the 200 one"
- "Same product, used wrong and used right"

**Situation**
- "I only found out how much this mattered on moving day"
- "Commuting at minus 10, this is the only thing I wear"

**Unboxing / process**
- "This came straight from the factory; here is what's inside"
- "From order to doorstep, day three"

**Numbers**
- "Three details decide whether it's worth the price"
- "Thirty days in, I changed three habits"

## Shot templates

**Single product demo (15s)**
1. Hook close-up (1s)
2. Wide shot in use (3s)
3. Three detail close-ups (2s each)
4. Comparison or test (4s)
5. Price + CTA (3s)

**Factory / supply chain (20s)**
1. Line or warehouse (3s)
2. Material or process close-up (5s)
3. Quality check or packing (4s)
4. The finished product in use (5s)
5. CTA (3s)

**Problem–solution (15s)**
1. Show the problem (3s)
2. The wrong fix (3s)
3. The right fix, with the product (6s)
4. CTA (3s)

## Platform differences

| | TikTok | Instagram Reels |
|---|---|---|
| Length | 15–34s works best | 15–30s |
| Pace | Faster; information in the first second | Can breathe; picture quality matters more |
| Captions | Required, large type | Required, clear of the bottom UI |
| Music | Trending audio on the platform | Trending audio, mind the rights |
| Caption text | Short, hook first | One more line of context is fine |
| Posting | 1–2 a day | 1 a day, quality first |

## Shooting requirements

- Vertical 9:16, 1080×1920 or larger
- Daylight or a ring light; never shoot into the light
- Clear sound matters more than a pretty picture; use a lav mic or voice over in noisy places
- One shot, one piece of information — do not pack three selling points into one shot

## Never

- Invent customer stories, before/after results or sales numbers
- Borrow someone else's footage, music or likeness
- Fake countdowns or fake promotions
- State an unverified benefit as fact

## Delivery format

For each topic: the hook, the shot list (one line per shot), the caption, the length, the platform, and the product it sells.
